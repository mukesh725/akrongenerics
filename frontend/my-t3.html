STATUTORY PRODUCT TRACING

What is T3 Data?

T3 refers to the three core components of product tracing data required under DSCSA regulations to verify that medications are authentic and handled safely at every stage of distribution:

Component 01 TI

Transaction Information (TI)

Detailed product and shipment data, including the proprietary or established drug name, strength, dosage form, National Drug Code (NDC), container size, number of containers, lot number, transaction date, shipment date, and the business names and addresses of both Akron Generics and the receiving pharmacy or healthcare facility.

  • Proprietary & established medication name
  • Strength, dosage form & container size
  • FDA 10/11-digit National Drug Code (NDC)
  • Batch/lot number & expiration dates
  • Exact shipment and delivery timestamps
  • Trading partner licensed entity addresses
Component 02 TH

Transaction History (TH)

A comprehensive record tracking the prior ownership history of the product back to the original manufacturer (applicable to legacy or non-serialized transactions).

  • Full prior ownership chain of custody
  • Original primary manufacturer provenance
  • Intermediary wholesale distribution lineage
  • Tamper-evident pedigree integrity
  • Applicable legacy transfer tracking
  • Board of Pharmacy audit-compliant logs
Component 03 TS

Transaction Statement (TS)

Formal attestation confirming that Akron Generics is an authorized trading partner under DSCSA, sourced the product from an authorized trading partner, did not knowingly ship suspect or illegitimate product, and maintains validated systems for product verification.

  • Authorized Trading Partner (ATP) attestation
  • Direct sourcing from certified partners
  • Non-suspect and legitimate product warranty
  • Validated verification & quarantine systems
  • Immediate illegitimate product reporting
  • Compliance with federal Title II mandates

REGULATORY ANSWERS

DSCSA & Traceability FAQs

Key details on DSCSA Title II interoperability, statutory retention, and receiving procedures for independent pharmacies.

What is the Drug Supply Chain Security Act (DSCSA)?
Enacted by Congress under Title II of the Drug Quality and Security Act (DQSA), the DSCSA outlines steps to build an electronic, interoperable system to identify and trace certain prescription drugs as they are distributed in the United States. Its primary goal is to protect consumers from drugs that may be counterfeit, stolen, contaminated, or otherwise harmful.
How long must pharmacies retain T3 product tracing data?
Under federal DSCSA regulations, dispensers (including independent retail pharmacies, hospital pharmacies, and clinics) must capture and maintain T3 product tracing records for a minimum of six (6) years from the transaction date. Akron Generics automatically maintains secure, audit-ready cloud archiving of all your T3 records for six years.
How do I connect our pharmacy system to Akron Generics via EPCIS?
Akron Generics utilizes modern, interoperable GS1 EPCIS (Electronic Product Code Information Services) standards through trusted compliance platforms such as LSPedia OneScan. If your pharmacy utilizes an automated compliance software (such as LSPedia, InfiniTrak, or TrackTraceRx), our compliance support team will establish your direct electronic data interchange (EDI/EPCIS) feed. Contact us at (631) 215-3009 for setup support.

This gives your staff immediate access to place wholesale orders, review catalog pricing, and access shipment track-and-trace files.
