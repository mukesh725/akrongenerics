About Akron Generics

Akron Generics is a family operated wholesale distributor which supplies prescription drugs, devices and over the counter products to hundreds of customers such as independent retail pharmacies, clinics, long term care pharmacies, chain stores, hospital and institutional pharmacies.

Akron Generics has a singular idea which is to concentrate on supporting our customers by providing a quality service and lowering pharmaceutical out-of-pocket costs for patients.

Our Board of members have over 50 years of collective experience in the pharmaceutical/health care industry. They have guided us to improve our quality of service and create a professional relationship with all of our customers & vendors.

Our 15,000 sq. ft. facility is located in the pharmaceutical hub of Commack, NY and is fully secured by 24/7 CCTV monitoring, keypad lock entrances and exits. This provides a safe work environment, secure and efficient pharmaceutical services to all our authenticated customers and vendors.
